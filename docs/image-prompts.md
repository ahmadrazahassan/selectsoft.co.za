# Image direction

The live site currently uses original CSS art so it remains fast, clear and free from stock photography. Product screenshots and official vendor logos must be sourced with permission and stored in Supabase media records.

## Hero still life

Create a high end editorial still life for an independent South African software publication. Show a bright worktable from a slightly raised angle, with an unbranded laptop, a printed comparison worksheet with unreadable lines, a calculator, a plain invoice folder and a deep blue pen. Use solid paper accents in deep blue, soft periwinkle and warm stone. Keep generous empty space and crisp natural daylight. No text, logo, interface, watermark, glow, gradient, decorative icon, staged handshake, flag, landmark or tourism motif.

## Accounting guide

Create a precise overhead editorial photograph of organised receipts, an unbranded calculator, ledger paper with unreadable marks and the corner of a laptop on a warm grey desk. Use one deep blue paper block and one restrained periwinkle accent. No text, logo, watermark, gradient or stock business pose.

## Payroll guide

Create an honest editorial workplace photograph of South African colleagues reviewing payroll records around a shared table. Keep people engaged with the work and not looking at the camera. Use an unbranded laptop, calm daylight, modest contemporary office details and a restrained stone, blue and periwinkle palette. No visible private information, text, logo, watermark or staged celebration.

## Comparison guide

Create a fair comparison still life with two equally sized unbranded notebooks facing each other across a thin centre rule. One notebook is deep blue and one is soft periwinkle. Give both the same pen and the same number of paper tabs. Use a warm grey surface, natural shadows and generous space. No text, logo, watermark, gradient or decorative technology motif.
