# Editorial workflow

## Research

Begin with primary vendor material, product documentation, current pricing, security information and local partner details. Save important sources with the product record. Never claim a structured trial or interview unless it happened.

## Draft

Write the verdict before the score. State who the product suits, what works well and what should make a buyer pause. Use the organisation's own records and process examples during any trial.

## Review

An editor checks every factual statement, primary link, score criterion, disclosure and South African context note. Material vendor corrections require evidence. The vendor does not approve the verdict.

## Publish

Complete search metadata, image text, author details, pricing check date and related content. Move the record to published only after the review is complete.

## Maintain

Recheck important reviews when pricing, ownership, support, availability or major features change. Record the editorial date and describe any substantial correction.
